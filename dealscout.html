How the deal score works

Each deal gets a 0–100 score from four weighted factors, computed on your financing assumptions:

  • Cap rate (35 pts) — 0 pts at 4%, full marks at 9%+.
  • Cash-on-cash (25 pts) — 0 at 0%, full at 12%+.
  • DSCR (25 pts) — 0 at 1.0×, full at 1.5×+.
  • Cash flow per unit (15 pts) — 0 at $0/mo, full at $150/unit/mo.

Grades: A 80+, B 65–79, C 50–64, D under 50. NOI comes from the listed cap rate on the board, or from the income you enter in Analyze. Max offer solves the price that still clears an 8% cash-on-cash and 1.25× DSCR at your terms.

DealScout — a screening & underwriting tool, not financial advice. Deal Board entries are unverified leads gathered from public marketplaces and can go stale; use Live Listings for current active inventory with broker contact, and verify rent roll, T‑12, taxes & insurance before making an offer.
